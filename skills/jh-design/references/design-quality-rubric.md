# Contextual Design Review

Use relevant questions, not a mandatory numerical score or fixed number of revision rounds.

- Does the page fit its audience and frequency of use? Is an operational app usable immediately rather than hidden behind promotional content?
- Is the next action clear, with input, result and secondary information distinguishable?
- Do Korean text, font weight, line breaks, spacing and contrast work at the actual viewing size?
- Are selected references visibly translated into this project's content and components, without copying their brand?
- Are normal, empty, error and loading states understandable where they exist?
- Does the affected layout work on mobile without clipping or overlap?
- Does motion help the task or identity, respect reduced motion and remain responsive? Skip this question for static work.
- Are source images recognizable and real claims supported? Are generated illustrations distinguishable from evidence?
- Were existing functionality, data and project conventions preserved?
- Were appropriate runtime/build checks and visual checks performed, with limitations stated?

Fix consequential defects. Do not create new scope solely to improve a rubric score. Stop when the requested result is usable and its important conditions have evidence.
